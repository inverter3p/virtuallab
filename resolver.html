Key equations

Vref(t) = sin(2π fexc t)
Vsin(t) = K sin(2π fexc t) sin θe(t)
Vcos(t) = K sin(2π fexc t) cos θe(t)
θe(t) = 2π tTrev   (1× resolver)

How to get angle

1
Demodulate Vsin and Vcos from the excitation carrier.
2
Normalize to remove gain / offset mismatch.
3
Compute angle using θ̂e = atan2(Vsin, Vcos).
4
Differentiate angle or use a PLL / tracking observer to estimate speed.

Observations

  • Resolver outputs are AM signals at the excitation frequency.
  • The envelopes of Vsin and Vcos are proportional to sin θe and cos θe.
  • Vsin and Vcos are in quadrature (90° phase difference).
  • For a 1× resolver, one complete envelope cycle occurs in one mechanical revolution.
  • Dragging the knob changes the rotor angle, and the plots reveal the waveforms progressively from 0 s to the current time.
Tip: Use the knob for manual exploration or press Play to animate one full revolution. The waveforms are generated from the exact equations shown above.

MOSFET Dynamic Switching Trajectories

Mapping the transition path through Triode and Saturation regions on the $i_D - V_{DS}$ plane.

1. Turn-On Trajectory Mapping

A. Delay: Point stays at ($V_{dc}, 0$) while $V_{GS}$ rises to $V_{th}$.
B. Current Rise: Point moves vertically from $0$ to $I_{load}$. $V_{GS}$ reaches $V_{pl}$.
C. Voltage Fall: Point moves horizontally from $V_{dc}$ to $V_{on}$ during the Miller Plateau.

2. Turn-Off Trajectory Mapping

A. Storage: Point stays at ($V_{on}, I_{load}$) as $V_{GS}$ falls from $V_{gate}$ to $V_{pl}$.
B. Voltage Rise: Point moves horizontally from $V_{on}$ to $V_{dc}$ at constant $I_{load}$.
C. Current Fall: Point moves vertically back to $0$ as $V_{GS}$ drops from $V_{pl}$ to $V_{th}$.
